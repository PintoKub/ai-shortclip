Privacy Policy — AI ShortClip

Last updated: 16 November 2026

AI ShortClip (“the app”) is a personal tool operated by its owner for the owner’s own accounts. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google OAuth scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): to read the owner’s channel name and upload videos the owner chooses to post.
  • TikTok (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish) and Instagram (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish): to show the account name and publish videos the owner chooses to post.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner’s own server, readable only by the owner’s system account. They are never sold, shared, or sent to third parties.
  • The app does not read, collect or store viewers’ data, comments, messages, or any other users’ information.
  • Videos are uploaded only after the owner reviews them and clicks “post”.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect any account in the app, or revoke access at Google account permissions, in TikTok settings, or in Instagram settings. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.
